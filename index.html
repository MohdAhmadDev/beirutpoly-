About Beirut Poly

Beirut Poly is a Monopoly-inspired board game that brings the vibrant streets, culture, and landmarks of Beirut to life. Players buy, trade, and develop iconic neighborhoods and attractions, from the historic Downtown Beirut to the bustling Hamra Street, while navigating the unique twists of city life.

Features

Explore Beirut

Visit iconic neighborhoods and landmarks like Downtown Beirut, Hamra Street, and the Corniche as you play.

Unique Tokens

Move around the board with Beirut-themed tokens like a cable car, coffee cup, ferris wheel, or cedar tree.

Fun & Strategic Gameplay

Experience strategic property management combined with cultural exploration, making the game perfect for locals and visitors alike.
